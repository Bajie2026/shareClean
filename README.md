# Support — ShareClean

ShareClean (App Store listing: "ShareClean: Redact Photos") is a fully
on-device photo redaction tool. You share an image from any app, review the
detected items, and continue sharing a masked copy. It works offline and
never uploads your photo.

## Quick answers

**Why does the system share sheet still preview the original image?**
The system share sheet may preview the original; the copy actually being
shared is the masked one. Check the "Masked & ready" confirmation before you
continue.

**Where is my masked copy?**
After you tap "Mask & Continue", a masked copy is placed on your clipboard
automatically. Long-press anywhere and paste it as a backup.

**Why do some items show up unchecked?**
Low-confidence guesses (such as names) are unchecked by default so you stay
in control. Turn them on manually if you want them masked too.

**I reached the daily free limit. Can I still redact?**
Yes. Core redaction is never locked. Exports after the daily free 3 include
a small corner mark; one-time Pro removes the mark and unlocks unlimited
exports.

**Purchases and refunds**
Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Use "Restore Purchases" on the
app's home screen to re-enable Pro, or contact Apple Support for billing
issues.
